`Samojed` brings another compact [Jazz & Bass](/labels/jazz-and-bass) release into the catalog, built around liquid drum & bass momentum, soft jazz phrasing, and a warm melodic core. The EP keeps the arrangements focused and clean, moving from the title track's smooth pressure into more reflective, late-night cuts.

For listeners looking for **jazzy liquid DnB** with polished drums, rounded bass movement, and atmospheric detail, `Samojed` works as both a short front-to-back listen and a practical source of rights-managed music for visual edits.
