`Caramel: Lofi Hip Hop and Drum & Bass Fusion` expands the [Chill Music Motif](/labels/chill-music-motif) catalog with a softer bridge between lofi hip hop texture and drum & bass movement. The EP keeps the melodies warm and approachable while adding enough rhythm to lift it beyond passive study music.

For listeners and creators looking for **lofi drum and bass** with mellow hip hop warmth, `Caramel` gives a compact five-track flow for focus, relaxed edits, and late-night listening.

## Tracklist

1. Caramel
2. No Worries
3. Sweaty Shirt
4. Let Me Cry
5. Past, Present, Future

## What you will hear

- Warm lofi hip hop textures with drum & bass-inspired rhythm
- Smooth melodic loops, gentle bass motion, and compact arrangements
- A mellow EP flow that works for focus without losing momentum

## Where it fits

- Study sessions, coding blocks, and quiet productivity playlists
- Relaxed lifestyle edits, cafe visuals, and mellow social content
- Drum & bass playlists that need a softer, lofi-leaning moment
