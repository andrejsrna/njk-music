`Calming Classroom Music` is a five-track [Calm Spirit Music](/labels/calm-spirit-music) EP that follows a school day from the first quiet minutes to the last bell. Soft pads, gentle piano and slow, unhurried movement keep the room calm without pulling attention away from the work.

It is made for teachers, parents and anyone who needs **calm background music** for reading time, independent work, drawing or the minutes when a group needs to settle down. The EP is not out on streaming platforms yet, but you can already preview every track here and download the lossless WAV files.

## Tracklist

1. Morning Arrival (4:20)
2. Quiet Reading Corner (4:25)
3. Focus Time (4:20)
4. Drawing by the Window (4:20)
5. Before the Bell (7:17)

## Where it fits

- **Morning Arrival**: a slow, bright start while everyone comes in and gets ready
- **Quiet Reading Corner**: the softest track on the EP, for reading and quiet time
- **Focus Time**: a gentle pulse for independent work and study blocks
- **Drawing by the Window**: a lighter melody for art, crafts and creative tasks
- **Before the Bell**: a longer, calm close for tidying up or winding down after a break
