`Calm Kettle: Micro Downtempo` expands the [Calm Spirit Music](/labels/calm-spirit-music) catalog with a warmer, rhythm-led EP built around soft deep-house motion and relaxed downtempo detail. The release keeps its pulse gentle enough for focus while giving each track enough structure to work as more than passive ambience.

For listeners and creators looking for **calm downtempo house** with clean textures, slow-burn grooves, and meditative warmth, this five-track set fits quiet productivity, lifestyle edits, and reflective listening sessions.

## Tracklist

1. Calm Kettle
2. Losing Myself
3. Right Hemisphere
4. Buzzy Speaker

## What you will hear

- Soft deep-house drums with relaxed micro downtempo pacing
- Warm pads, subtle melodic figures, and calm low-end movement
- A focused EP flow that supports concentration without feeling static

## Where it fits

- Calm work sessions, journaling playlists, and late-night focus blocks
- Spa, wellness, cafe, and lifestyle environments that need gentle rhythmic energy
- Reflective visual edits, travel fragments, and low-pressure creator backdrops
